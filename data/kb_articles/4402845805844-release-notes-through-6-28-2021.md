# Release Notes Through 6/28/2021

## 

## Portal Updates:

**Portal:**

    * Support for different weight UOM on the shipment details page
    * Ability to subscribe to notifications for product feed failures (the video below shows how to subscribe for this notification)


  *     * 


**Inventory:**

  * Retail price can be made visible on the Inventory Search page using the show columns selection
  * Retail price updated in the Standard Feed upload



## Connector Updates:

  * **DSCO** : 
    * Support for sending large batches for inventory
    * Add invoice fields for Nordstrom
  * **NetSuite** : Commerce EDI Bundle
    * Add First and Last name fields to addresses if they are not a duplicate of the CompanyName field
  * **Shopify** : 
    * Weight and UOM displayed on orders 
      * Acceptable UOM is grams 
    * Link to packing slips are accessible in the note field when posted to Shopify
  * **ShipStation** : Weight and Weight UOM mapped on the order (the video below shows where the weight appears) 
    * Supported UOM options: pounds, ounces, or grams



## Bug Fixes:

  * **Overdue Document Reports Performance** : Reporting goes back 180 days and queries were modified
