# Go live

## 

### **Request to go live**

Once you have completed onboarding steps, click on **Request to go live**. Your retailer**** will review and reject or approve your request. 

### **Your retailer will push your account live**

Your retailer will confirm all onboarding steps are complete and will push your account into production. You will receive an email with the subject line **"[Supplier]'s connection with [Retailer]********is live!"** This email will link you to our production portal.

### **Complete post-production steps**

Once you are live, you will need to complete post-production onboarding steps. Any settings added in stage will not carry over so these will need to be re-configured in production:

  1. Set up your connection in production 
     * **API Suppliers** : Generate a production API key from the [**API Authentication**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/profile/api-authentication) page
     * **EDI Suppliers** : Add your production EDI connection from the [**EDI Connection**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/settings/edi) page
     * **Shopify Suppliers** : Add your production Shopify connection from the [**Connections**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/connections) page 
     * **ShipStation Suppliers** : Add your production ShipStation connection from the [**Connections**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/connections) page 
  2. Upload/send a live product feed from the [**Product Feeds**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/product-feeds) page (if required) 
  3. Upload/send live inventory in production from the [**Inventory Feeds**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/advanced-product-management) page
  4. Upload a matching file in production from the [**Inventory Feeds**](https://portal.logicbroker.com/advanced-product-management) page (if required) 
  5. Re-configure any stage settings in the production portal
