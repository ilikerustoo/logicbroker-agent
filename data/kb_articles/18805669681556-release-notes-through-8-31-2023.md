# Release Notes Though: 8/31/2023

## 

## Portal:

  * **Onboarding:**
    * Enhancements to the _Retailer Onboarding Form_ , _Request to Go Live Rejected_ , _Form Rejected_ , _Onboarding Terminated_ , and _Live_ emails sent to Suppliers
    * Updates to  _Request to Go Live_ email sent to Retailers
    * Enhanced filtering options 
    * Enhanced individual supplier onboarding information page
    * Bulk supplier invite and single invite are accessible through the "invite supplier" button
    * Support for removing supplier integration once deployed to stage
    * Status Updates: 
      * Tooltip to define statuses 
      * More intuitive onboarding statuses (color-coded)
    * More streamlined go-live flow for retailers 
    * Retailer Onboarding setting manager: 
      * Support for customized retailer messages in the portal for suppliers to view
      * Ability to use tags for categorization
      * Updated Supplier agreement design
  * **Order Management:**
    * Allow duplicates to replace an existing document if the document is ignored or failed. Only applicable to non-order documents at this time.
    * Switch dimensions on shipping details to LxWxH
    * Support for PartnerSKU on bulk pick filter and picklist
  * **Scorecards** : Ability to filter by grade 
  * **EDI** : functionality to support consolidated shipments (shipping multiple POs on an 856)



## Connectors: 

  * **Channel Advisor** : Save Channel Advisor's orderId in extended attributes as "ChannelAdvisorOrderId"
  * **Shopify:** Pull cost on orders



## Fixes: 

  * Updated code for portal invitations to prevent from showing as invalid or expired to suppliers
