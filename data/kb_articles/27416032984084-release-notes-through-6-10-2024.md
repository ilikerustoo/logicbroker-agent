# Release Notes Though: 6/10/2024

## 

Portal:

  * **Connected Commerce Network®:** Revamped homepage to expand assortment through new trading partners 
    * Collect profile information on the onboarding form to display in the network
  * **Manage Users (My Profile):** Update settings/manage permission to enable updates to the profile shown through the Connected Commerce Network® 
  * **Product Feeds:** Export to VFTP with UTF8 encoding
  * **Product Onboarding Center:** Improved error messaging on retailer export 
    * Show full path on the product export file



Connectors: 

  * **Shopify:** Supplier configuration to continue selling when out of stock 
    * Support to pull barcode on orders
  * **Squarespace (Supplier Flow):** This supplier flow connector accepts orders, and provides acknowledgements, shipments, inventory, and product catalog.
  * **BigCommerce Connector** : Configurable filter to pull orders into the system



Fixes:

  * Mapping issue for Product Onboarding Center during supplier import
  * Update the flag on the profile edit banner to show only to suppliers
  * Page loading issues with the KVPList attributes
