# General Information - FAQ's

## 

In this article, you will find some of the frequently asked questions from users when they are getting started with using Logicbroker.

### **Logicbroker Account Creation**

**Can't find the portal invitation "Welcome to Logicbroker!" email?**

_Check your junk mail folder & add _[ _do-not-reply@logicbroker.com_](mailto:do-not-reply@logicbroker.com) _to your contacts list._

_Someone from your team can send you an invitation. Instructions are linked[here](/kb/logicbroker/360021857352-Manage-Users?hsLang=en)._

_Still no luck? Reach out to[support@logicbroker.com](mailto:support@logicbroker.com) and let us know your account name and your email and we'll send you a new invitation. _

**Missed the 7-day deadline to register?**

_If someone from your team has an account they can send you a new invitation. Instructions are linked[here](/kb/logicbroker/360021857352-Manage-Users?hsLang=en)._

_Still no luck? Reach out to[support@logicbroker.com](mailto:support@logicbroker.com) and let us know your account name and your email and we'll send you a new invitation. _

**Already registered but can't log in?**

_Make sure you are using your username to log in, not your email (unless you registered with your email address as your username). Note that usernames and passwords are case-sensitive._

_If you cannot remember your username, you can ask someone from your team who has**Users** permissions (most have this turned on by default): in the portal, go to **Settings** > **Manage Users**_

_Still no luck? Reach out to[support@logicbroker.com](mailto:support@logicbroker.com) and let us know your account name and your email address and we'll remind you of your username._

**Trying to log in but your company is not enabled in production?**

_Depending on your integration method, your company may or may not be enabled in production during the onboarding phase - when trying to login into the production portal, you will see an error message._

_If you are seeing that error message, reach out to[support@logicbroker.com](mailto:support@logicbroker.com) for more information. _

_***Please Note:**__your credentials for stage will be the same as for production._

**Want to invite someone from your team to the Logicbroker portal?**

_You can send them an invitation from the portal. Instructions are linked[here](/kb/logicbroker/360021857352-Manage-Users?hsLang=en)._

_***Please Note:**__turning on all permissions will provide better accessibility in the future_
