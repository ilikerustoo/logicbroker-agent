# Manage Users

## 

**Audience :** All users in Logicbroker. 

In this article, you will find information related to the **Manage Users** **Page**. The sections within this article are outlined below:

  * At a Glance
  * Actions



### **At a Glance**

The **Manage Users** page provides users with the ability to see and manage active account users. Logicbroker credentials provide access to: [Stage Portal](https://stageportal.logicbroker.com/), [Production Portal](https://portal.logicbroker.com/) and [Learn Logicbroker](https://learn.logicbroker.com/).

### **Actions**

The **Manage Users** page provides users with a variety of actions that can be taken on users. _**Please note:**_ To add, manage or export users, you must have the **users/manage** permission enabled on your account.

**Invite a new user**

1\. Type in the name **email address** of the user you want to invite - you can add up to 50 emails separated by commas, spaces, or semicolons 

2\. Adjust the **permissions** \- by default, **orders/manage** and **inventoy/manage** will be enabled 

3\. **Send invite**

4\. The user will receive an email notification with the **Subject Line: "Welcome to Logicbroker!** " where they will create their username and password - this invitation will allow the new user to create a login and password to the Logicbroker platform. If you d on’t see the email, check your junk mail folder and add [do-not-reply@logicbroker.com](mailto:do-not-reply@logicbroker.com) to your contact list _**Please note:**_ Invitations expire within 7 days - reach out to your account manager or [support@logicroker.com](mailto:support@logicroker.cm) to have the invitation resent   
  


5\. Once the user has activated their account, they will appear in the  **Users** section

**Modify or delete a user**

1\. Scroll down to **Users** and locate the user you want to modify or delete 

2\. Click on **Edit** or **Delete**

3\. If modifying, you can modify **First Name** , **Last Name** , and **Permissions** \- you cannot modify username or email address. Delete the user and have them register again with their new information.   
  


**Export**

1\. Scroll down to **Users**

2\. Click on **Export**

3\. The export will include:   
  


  * Username
  * Email
  * First Name
  * Last Name
  * Permissions
