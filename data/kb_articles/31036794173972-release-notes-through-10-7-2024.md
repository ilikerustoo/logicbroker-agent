# Release Notes Through 10/7/2024

## 

Portal:

  * **Connected Commerce Network®: Ability to quickly find partners who are considered top-sellers based on performance over the last 30 days using badges**
    * **Enhancements to performance filters to help with usability**
    * **Reformatted icons on the supplier cards to help with page responsiveness**
  * **Product Onboarding Center:** Support for retailer catalog delivery using sFTP 
    * Ability for retailers to import products into UI to assist with enhancing catalog data and bringing it back into the portal
    * Support for simultaneous product imports in the UI 
    * Drag and drop image reordering in the portal
    * Check for ParentSKU uniqueness
    * Updated category template to improve supplier experience for optional fields
  * **Listings Management:** Workflow to pull products from supplier catalog when posting to marketplaces 
    * Showcase channels on the left side of the product page with inventory quantity 
    * Ability to list and delist from the product page 



Connectors: 

  * **Shopify:** Added the check products button on the connector for suppliers to see total products, total products tagged, active and available to import, number of duplicate SKUs and number of missing SKUs
  * **Squarespace:** Added the check products button on the connector for suppliers to see total products, total products active and available to import, number of duplicate SKUs and number of missing SKUs
  * **Lazada:** New cancellation endpoint that now uses GET /order/reverse/cancel/validate and GET /order/reverse/cancel/create
