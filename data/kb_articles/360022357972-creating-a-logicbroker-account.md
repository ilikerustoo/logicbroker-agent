# Creating a Logicbroker Account

## 

**Audience** : All new users in Logicbroker. 

In this article, you will find information to help with creating your Logicbroker account. The sections within this article are outlined below:

  * Overview
  * Welcome to Logicbroker - Email Link 
  * Related Content



### **Overview**

Logicbroker will generate an invitation for the first user via email to create a Logicbroker account for their company. 

If a company has existing users, a company administrator has the ability to generate the invitation directly in the portal. For more information on how to invite new users as an administrator for your company, please visit the Manage Users article linked [here](/kb/logicbroker/360021857352-Manage-Users?hsLang=en).

### **"Welcome to Logicbroker"**

When the email is received, the subject line will read “Welcome to Logicbroker”. The email will contain a link prompting users to provide the information needed to set up an account including: 

  * Username 
  * Name 
  * Password – this should be at least 10 characters/digits in length 



The provided link will expire 7 days after receipt of this email. 

**_Please note:_**_You should also check your spam folders in case the email was routed there._
