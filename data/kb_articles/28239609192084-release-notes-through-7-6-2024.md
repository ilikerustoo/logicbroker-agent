# Release Notes Though: 7/6/2024

## 

Portal:

  * **Connected Commerce Network®:** Showcase featured suppliers on the main landing page 
  * **Inventory:** Support for SKUs with commas 
  * **Onboarding:** Include Product Onboarding Center as a core onboarding step
  * **Testing:** Support for export functionality 
  * **Product Onboarding Center:** Ability to filter by partner SKU 
    * Support for matching inventory and products in bulk
    * Set default filter to Last Update on the Supplier Catalog
    * Advanced mapping tools to help with data transformations 
    * Improved performance for extra large taxonomies 
  * **Partner Management Center:** Support for a partner management module where retailers can collect additional information, pause suppliers, and review hours of operation for their supplier partners 
    * Ability to pause suppliers based on performance, inaccurate inventory, etc. in the portal 
    * Enhanced supplier profile to view more extensive data and scorecards in a single location
    * Support for an event log to understand specific actions, particularly around pausing suppliers 
    * Profile enhancements to collect warehouse closures, hours of operation, and locations
    * Support for additional contacts within the organization 
    * **[API Endpoints:](https://stage.commerceapi.io/swagger/ui/index#/Partner)**
      * POST /api/v2/Partners/CompanyProfile/ - 'Create partner company profile' (supplier only)

      * GET /api/v2/Partners/CompanyProfile/{partnerId} 'Get partner company profile' (retailer only)

      * DELETE /api/v2/Partners/CompanyProfile 'Delete company profile' (supplier only)




Connectors: 

  * **Faire** : Marketplace connector that accepts shipments, acknowledgments, and inventory. This connector provides orders.
  * **Marketplacer** : Send smaller batches on product feed
  * **WooCommerce:** Do not set manage_stock to true for products with variants
  * **Shopify** : Pull product based on last update time instead of batch time 
    * Preserve variant position on product import



Fixes:

  * Products that failed to list and have been edited were indicating errors, updated to remove error icon
  * Save button now working after editing a product in My Catalog
  * Bulk Approve automatically Lists Product, updated to go into the approved status 
  * Users are no longer prompted to need to click save when nothing has changed
  * Update images so they are displaying in the correct order in Product Onboarding Center
