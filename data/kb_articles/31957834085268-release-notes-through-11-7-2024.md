# Release Notes Through 11/7/2024

## 

Portal:

**Product Onboarding Center:** Support for suppliers to leverage automatch on attribute mapping

  * Enhanced supplier import template to hide unnecessary attributes and include additional dropdowns when applicable 
  * Support to showcase duplicate SKUs during the import process 



**Partner Management Center:** CSV report to show paused suppliers 

Connectors: 

**Walmart: U.S. Partnership to provide marketplace sellers with the ability to receive and fulfill orders and send product data **

  * **Walmart DSV: Set the URL to post cancellations correctly**



**Shopify: Support the ability for retailers to use their own order Ids **

  * **Pull variant attributes on the order line level**



**TikTok: Support for suppliers to sell products on TikTok in the United States. Connector supports orders, cancellations, returns, shipments, invoices, and inventory.**

**Edenred: Supplier connector that accepts orders and provides acknowledgements/cancellations, shipments, inventory and product catalog. **

**WooCommerce: Product images to showcase variant images **

****Squarespace** : Filter on Last Modified Date when Pulling Products**

# Fixes:

**Connected Commerce Network: Suppliers who are opting out are still showing in the network**

**Product Onboarding Center** : 

  * Manual approval prompt showing for already matched parent SKUs
  * Encode SKU in URL on inventory and catalog search
  * Bulk approve modal not showing when trying to approve products
