# Release Notes Through 11/30/2020

## 

## Portal Updates:

  * **EDI** : New workflow for 860s, users will need to accept the 860 before it can be applied.
  * **EDI** : New fields are now visible for 860/865s: 
    *       * Requested Ship Date
      * Estimated Delivery Date
      * Update Request Number
  * **ShipFromAddress** will be automatically copied from shipment to invoice
  * **Connections** are now available through the Logicbroker Portal under **Settings**. Users are able to input their credentials directly in the Portal once obtained from their connector.



## Connector Updates:

  * **Shopify:** Location ID is now visible in the Portal through the Connections feature
