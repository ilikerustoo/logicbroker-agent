# Release Notes thru 6/20/2018

## 

**New Features:**

  * Ability to upload and download inventory feeds in Excel format.
  * Inventory validation available for retailers specifications.
  * Integration with SKU Vault to process order, shipments and send inventory.
  * Suppliers can view their retailer's vendor number on the Account information page
  * Add Item "Status" on inventory updates sent to retailers.
