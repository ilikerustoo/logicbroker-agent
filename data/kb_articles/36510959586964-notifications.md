# Notifications

## 

**Audience :** All users in Logicbroker. 

In this article, you will find information related to the **Notifications** **Page**. The sections within this article are outlined below:

  * At a Glance
  * Defaults
  * Scheduled Reports
  * Filters
  * Definitions



### **At a Glance**

The **Notifications** page provides users with the ability to see and manage their email notifications and set filters. _**Please note**_ : These notifications are set on a user-by user basis. 

### **Defaults**

All users will be set up with default notifications initially (see below). Retailers can also set up default notifications on behalf of their suppliers by contacting [support@logicbroker.com ](mailto:support@logicbroker.com)and sharing the list of notifications they want enabled for all their suppliers. Users will still be able to disable or unsubscribe once they are in the portal. 

### **Scheduled Reports**

All the notifications with a blue info icon require a scheduled report to be configured. Click the info icon to see what specific report you need to configure from the **Scheduled Reports** page under the **Reporting** page. _**Please note**_ : To access the **Scheduled Reports** page, you will need a Logicbroker subscription. 

### **Filters**

The filters section allows users to filter the notifications they receive. This is helpful to reduce the number of emails received and ensure only important notifications are received. See below an example of how a user can set up a filter to receive new order alerts for a specific parter. 

### **Definitions**

See below for what each notification will provide along with information on if it is enabled by default for users. 

**Information**

---|---|---  
**New order** |  Provides a count of all new orders received in the past hour. Will include a link to view the new orders in the Portal.  Provides an alert to the first sales order from a retailer and the first purchase order between partners sent to both the retailer and supplier.  |   
**New shipment** |  Provides a count of all new shipments received in the past hour. Will include a link to view the new shipments in the Portal.  |   
**New invoice** |  Provides a count of all new invoices received in the past hour. Will include a link to view the new invoices in the Portal. |   
**New return** |  Provides a count of all new returns received in the past hour. Will include a link to view the new returns in the Portal.  |   
**Order cancellation** |  Triggered when an order is cancelled. |  ✅  
**Monitoring alert** |  Enables monitoring reports with thresholds configured under the Monitoring page. To learn more about monitoring configurations see the monitoring section below. |  ✅  
**Daily activity summary** |  Will provide a daily a list daily of total documents transmitted through the system, with a breakdown of Total Orders, Open Orders, Complete Orders, Total Shipments, and Total Invoices. This can be configured in the Monitoring section, no parameters are required for configuration. |   
**Monthly document count** |  Will send once a month including all information breakdown of all documents sent in that month. This includes # of orders, shipments and invoices. Must setup the **Monthly Document Report** under Monitoring to receive email notification. |   
**Monthly order count** |  Will send once a month and include the total number of orders received for that month. Must setup the **Monthly Order Report** to under Monitoring to receive email notification. |   
**FTP activity (file upload/delete)** |  Logicbroker monitors all SFTP/FTP activity on the hosted on the platform. If a user uploads, deletes, or edits a document on the SFTP/FTP server the username, company id, IP address, and directory the action took place will be logged in the email. This is to track when users are logging in and verify when files are downloaded. |   
**Unknown EDI documents** |  Will trigger when an EDI document is received for your account an your partner's identifiers do not match any partners you are connected to. |  ✅  
**Failed document report** |  Need to configure under the Monitoring to setup a full list of all documents that are in a Failed status within the system. This includes Orders, Acknowledgements, Shipments, and Invoices. |  ✅  
**Inventory update report** |  Need to configure under the Monitoring page. Subscribing will provide an inventory update summary for all your partners once every 24 hours. It will include Total Items, Total Updates, Matched Items, Matched Updates, Unmatched Items and In Stock % for each partner. |  ✅  
**Unmatched inventory report** |  Need to configure under the monitoring page. Will provide an update every 24 hours with all partners unmatched items count. |  ✅  
**Inventory activity** |  Will provide an update every time inventory is uploaded by one of your partners. This will include number of items and the link to the file uploaded to download. |   
**Document aging report** |  Will need to configure under the monitoring page. Will provide a full list of all partners and order counts for all documents not complete with their aging. Ranges include < 7 days, 7-14 days, 14-30 days, 30-60 days, and >60 days. The report can be configured to run daily at midnight or morning. |  ✅  
**Trading partner notification** |  Email will be sent when a trading partner sends a message. The message can be sent through the messaging center or through EDI like an 864 text message. In addition, if a document is received that does not map to a standard Logicbroker Document Type (Order, Acknowledgement, Shipment, Invoice, Return or Inventory) a notification will be sent with details as well. |  ✅  
**Score card** |  Will provide a weekly score card for all your partners. This will include overall score, shipment time, shipment % and cancel %. |   
**Routing instructions** |  Available if using routing instructions (for EDI this is the 754) with your channel/retailer. Notification will provide refernce numbers, shipment pickup time, carrier, and carrier contact information. |   
**GDPR notification** |  Will provide an update of all redacted documents related to your account. |   
**Testing completed** |  Will provide an update when testing has been completed for your partner. |   
  
**Errors**

Notification | Description | Default  
---|---|---  
**SKU not found** |  Used if connected to a system and a SKU couldn’t be found when inserting a document into your system. |  ✅  
**Order sourcing failed** |  Used if using Logicbroker's purchase order routing logic. Occurs when a supplier can not be found to create a purchase order off of a sales order received. |  ✅  
**Business rule failed** |  Triggered if a business rule fails to run, this will typically contain complex validation logic for all documents sent or received. |   
**Failed to update shipping information** |  Sent when shipments failed to update on the order. This occurs when items do not match the original order. |   
**Tracking upload failed** |  Triggered when an a bulk CSV/XLSX upload is made in the portal (under Order Management) and the upload fails. Will provide detailed information for the failure reason. |  ✅  
**External XML translation alert** |  Triggered if you are using a custom XML format and translation fails to create the document inside the Logicbroker system. |   
**Inventory error** |  Will provide an email if you or your partner attempted to process and inventory update and it failed. Notification will provide a link to download the file and details on why the update failed. |  ✅  
**API error** |  Triggered if you are integrating using an API connection to a system and Logicbroker fails to connect to that system. |   
**Scheduled task failed** |  Triggered if there is a scheduled job setup on your account and fails to process. This can include a job scheduled to pick up files from an external SFTP/FTP site or sending data to a configured connection; for example sending inventory updates on a schedule. |   
**Document validation failed** |  Triggered when a document either sent or received fails validation from wither you or your partners requirements. Full details will be provided in the email. |   
  
**Warnings**

Notification | Description | Default  
---|---|---  
**Login to external system failed** |  Triggered if you are connected to a system using a pre-built connector and Logicbroker receives a connection error. The connection will continue to retry, however the warning is sent with full details for visibility to potential issues. |   
**Overdue acknowledgments** |  Will provide a list of all orders that are awaiting functional acknowledgements (997), this is set to orders that haven't been acknowledged over 24 hours. |  ✅  
**Overdue documents** |  Configured under the Monitoring page. Will provide a list of documents that are still open. Similar to the aging report however will provide a list of open orders, with a maximum of 50 in each report. |  ✅  
**Overdue inventory** |  Provides a list of all partners who haven't provided inventory in over 24 hours. Total Items, Matched Items, and Unmatched Items are also provided. |  ✅
