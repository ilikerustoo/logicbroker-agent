# Release Notes Through 1/31/2025

## 

Portal:

**Product Onboarding Center:** Support for importing products based on specific channels and configure settings based on the channel  
  
**POC** : Listings Dashboard is now accessible

**POC** : Import UI enhancements to include a mapping button updates and new rule creation dialogue

**POC** : Show inventory in the product table for suppliers/sellers

**POC** : Import renames and upload enhancement to include UI updates, support mapping and edits on a company level and include source system

**Onboarding** : Automated reminder messages for retailers to be able to configure to remind suppliers automatically when they have not completed the onboarding form or continued with onboarding steps

Messaging/Communication: 

**Order Cancellation email Notification:** will now include shipping and billing address along with customer emails

Connectors: 

**Shopify: Support for approving and listing multi-variant products piecemeal**

**POC** : SKUs timing out in POC
