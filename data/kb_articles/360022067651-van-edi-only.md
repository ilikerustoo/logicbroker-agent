# VAN (EDI Only)

## 

Logicbroker has the ability to transmit your EDI documents to your VAN provider. Our platform uses a 3rd party provider **ECGRID** for all VAN communication. 

To get started you will need to provide the following:

  * **VAN Provider**
  * **ISA ID - for all document types**
  * **GS ID - for all document types**



You can provide this in the onboarding survey that was sent to you or [contact support](mailto:support@logicbroker.com).

####  __ Note:

When setting up a VAN connection, there will be additional charges associated with it. It is always recommended to update your connection to use either AS2 or SFTP/FTP to save on processing costs.
