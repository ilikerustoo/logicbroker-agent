# Release Notes Through 3/2/2021

## 

## Portal Updates:

  * Support for Message Level Encryption for FTP/SFTP integrations.
  * The order quantity shipped and cancelled will update when shipments or acknowledgements are ignored.



## Learn Logicbroker Updates:

  * Faceted search has been applied to the learning platform to organize and filter search results.
  * Tables will now be used to organize information such as specifications, codes, statuses, etc.



## Connector Updates:

  * **Wayfair** : Delta inventory feeds are required to be split into groups of 100,000 items.
  * **Walmart** : Visibility of estimated ship and delivery date on orders.
  * **Amazon** : Seller Central shipments and Amazon Direct documents will now support 24 hour instead of 12 hour time.
