# Release Notes Through 12/24/2020

## 

Portal Updates: 

  * **Address fields** are now editable when an order **fails**.
  * [Validation rules](/kb/logicbroker/360054359431-Document-Standards-Validation-Rules?hsLang=en) are available in the Logicbroker Portal under **Document Standards** located in the **Supplier (Retailer)** function. Users are able to view their partners' required fields when sending documents.
  * **Alert messages** are now linked on the Dashboard and when selected, will be filtered to only display messages where action is required. 
  * When **testing** warehouses, test cases will now display **vendor numbers** and warehouse codes. 

Connector Updates: 
  * **NetSuite:** Supplier and Commerce EDI bundles now have a field to store error messages.
  * **ShipStation:** Order date is now visible on acknowledgements.

  * **ChannelAdvisor** : Orders will only be pulled where the payment status is cleared.
