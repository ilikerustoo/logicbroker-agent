# Onboarding Page

## 

** Audience: ** Retailers using the On-Demand Onboarding process.

In this article, you will find information related to the **Onboarding** page . The sections within this article are outlined below:

  * Prerequisites
  * Onboarding Page 
  * Vendor Integrations Page 
  * Process Overview 



**Prerequisites**

**Implement On-Demand Onboarding**   
The **Onboarding** page is only available to retailers that are using our On-Demand Onboarding process. To find out more and see how this process would work with your organization, reach out to your Client Success Manager. This service is free of charge but does require additional setup from Logicbroker. 

* * *

**Set up permissions**   
You must have the permissions: **integrations/manage** enabled to see this page - see **[Manage Users](/kb/logicbroker/360021857352-Manage-Users?hsLang=en) ** for more information on how to get setup

* * *

**Log in using the stage portal**   
The  **Onboarding** page is only available in the **stage** **portal** as all onboarding tasks take place in stage so be sure you are using the correct portal. 

### 

###  **Onboarding Page**

The **Onboarding** page is where retailers are able to initiate, manage and see all their new vendor onboardings. Retailers can take action on vendor accounts to push them along the process. 

**At a Glance**

**Company name**

The name of the vendor   


* * *

**Primary contact**

The primary contact for the vendor - this is the person the **New Integration** email will be sent to

* * *

**Status**

The status of the vendor account - this field will not indicate where in the onboarding process the vendor is in. Status codes include:   


  * **Form Open** \- the retailer has sent that **New Integration** email
  * **Form Completed** \- the vendor has completed the **Integration Form**
  * **Deployed to Stage** \- the retailer has accepted the **Integration Form**
  * **Live** \- the retailer has pushed the vendor account into production



* * *

**Priority**

Visibility into the priority the retailer has for the vendor. Priority levels include: 

  * **Low**
  * **Medium**
  * **High**
  * **Ignored** \- this priority level is used to ignore a vendor integration since once deployed to stage, integrations cannot be deleted



* * *

**Started**

The date and time stamp of when the retailer initiated the process for the new vendor onboarding

* * *

**Requested Go Live**

The date and time stamp of when the retailer requested the vendor to go live - this comes from the new vendor invitation

* * *

**Live**

The date and time stamp of when the vendor was pushed into production 

**Display**

**Show/hide columns**

Click on the 3 vertical lines in the top right to customize your view. Toggle on (red) to add a column to your **Integrations** page _ **Please Note:** _ these settings will not stick and will need to be reconfigured if you refresh the screen   
  


* * *

**Filter results**

Click on the 3 horizontal lines in the top right to filter your results. Select the column you want to filter by, the operator, the results you want to display then click **Add** . Once a filter is added, you will see a button with that filter in the top left. Filters can be stacked, so add new filters or remove them by clicking the **x** in t filter button up top.

**Supplier Agreement**

The **Supplier Agreement** is an optional tool retailers can use as part of their vendor onboarding process. This document can be used to share vendor agreements or terms and conditions with the vendor at the beginning of the onboarding process. This is mostly used in marketplace scenarios as dropship tends to have more redlining and negotiations between parties.

* * *

**How it works**

If the retailer decides to use the **Supplier Agreement** , they will upload one into the portal as a PDF document. The same document will be shared with all vendors, so be sure this document is standard across all vendors. Once the retailer initiates a new vendor onboarding, the vendor will receive the **New Integration** email where they will be prompted to fill out the **Integration Form**. As part of the form, they will see the **Supplier Agreement** which they can read through, download and sign. Vendors cannot move onto the next step without signing the agreement which ensures all vendors are aligned with the set policies. 

* * *

**View Supplier Agreement**

To see the agreement that is in place, click on the **View Supplier Agreement** button 

* * *

**Upload Supplier Agreement**

To upload a new agreement or update an existing one, click on the **Upload Supplier Agreement** button 

**Onboard a new vendor**

There are two ways retailers can onboard a new vendor, in bulk or individually:

* * *

**Bulk Invite**

Bulk invitations can be sent to up to 50 new vendors at a time. 

  1. Click on the **Bulk Invite** button   
  
  

  2. Download the template and fill out: 
     * ***Company** \- _required_ \- do not include vendor or account ID numbers
     * ***Email** \- _required_ \- this email must be unique, you cannot use the same email that you used for another vendor integration
     * **Priority** \- _optional_ \- this field is only visible to the retailer in the **Integrations** page (Allowed values are: low, medium, high)
     * **Requested Go Live** \- _optional_ \- this will be reflected on the **New Integration** email and is meant to set an expectation for the vendor   
  

  3. Save your file and upload it
  4. Click **Send Invites**
  5. Review results and fix any errors   

  6. See the new integrations in the **Integrations** page   




* * *

**New Integration**

New integrations are sent to one vendor at a time.   


  1. Click on the **New Integration** button   
  
  

  2. Fill out: 
     * ***Company** \- _required_ \- do not include vendor or account ID numbers
     * ***Email** \- _required_ \- this email must be unique, you cannot use the same email that you used for another vendor integration
     * **Priority** \- _optional_ \- this field is only visible to the retailer in the **Integrations** page (Allowed values are: low, medium, high)
     * **Requested Go Live** \- _optional_ \- this will be reflected on the **New Integration** email and is meant to set an expectation for the vendor   

  3. Click **Send Invite**
  4. See the new integrations in the **Integrations** page   




* * *

**View**

To see more details on a vendor and updates on their integration, click on the **View** button. 

* * *

**Delete**

To delete a vendor integration, click on the **Delete** button.  **Note** : You can only delete integrations in a status of **Form Open**. Once vendors are **Deployed to Stage** , you cannot delete their integration since we cannot remove accounts that have been deployed. In this case, you can set the priority to **Ignored**. 

### 

###  **Vendor Integration Page**

Click on **View** on any of the vendors to see the **Vendor Integration** page. This page displays details on a specific vendor including status, form details and account updates. 

**At a Glance**

**Form details**

The top half of the page will display the vendor's **Integration Form** details    


* * *

**Account history**

The bottom half of the page will display updates on the account - these will not be specific to onboarding steps

**Actions**

**Change Priority**

Retailers can change the **priority** for any vendor if priorities shift within their organization. These updates will be displayed on the main page.    


* * *

**Change Requested Live Date**

Retailers can change the requested live date for any vendor as dates shift. These updates will be displayed on the main page.  **Note** : Changing this field will not notify the vendor of the date change.

* * *

**Accept/Reject**

Once the vendor submits the Integration Form, the retailer will receive an email notification (as long as they have the **Trading Partner Notification** enabled). In the **Onboarding** page, the retailer will review the vendor's submission and either accept or reject it.  **Note** : These actions are only available when the vendor's status is **Form Completed**. 

  * **If rejected** : the vendor will receive an email notification with the rejection reason and the opportunity to go back and edit the information
  * **If accepted** : the vendor account will be deployed to stage and they will receive 2 emails: 
    * **Welcome to Logicbroker!** \- this email will allow the vendor to create a username and password - it expires in 7 days
    * **Connection with Retailer is ready for testing** \- this email will direct the vendor to the stage portal to begin testing



* * *

**Resend Invite**

If the vendor did not review or receive the **Welcome to Logicbroker!** email which links them to create a username and password before the 7 day expiration period, the retailer can issue a new email by clicking the **Resend Invite** button.  **Note** : This action is only available when the vendor's status is **Deployed in Stage**.

* * *

**Go Live**

Once the vendor has completed all onboarding tasks (these vary depending on the retailer), the retailer can deploy the vendor's account into production by clicking the Go Live button.  **Note** : This action is only available when the vendor's status is **Deployed in Stage**. 

### 

###  **Process Overview**

This section will go over the process of onboarding a vendor using the **Onboarding** page. 

**Process Overview**

1\. Retailer sends out the new vendor invitation in the portal by clicking on the **Bulk Invite** or **New Integration** buttons.   
**Status** : Form Open

* * *

2\. Vendor receives the **New Integration** email 

* * *

3\. From the **New Integration** email, the vendor clicks on the link to be directed to the **Integration** Form. This form will ask the vendor:

  *     * If they are using an existing account or creating a new one   
  
  

    * Company information (only fields marked with an asterisk are required)   
  
  

    * Sign the **Supplier Agreement** (only if the retailer uploaded one to their account)   
  
  

    * Review and submit 



**Status** : Form Completed

* * *

4\. Once the vendor completes the **Integration Form** , the retailer will receive a **Form Completed** email (as long as they have the **Trading Partner Notification** enabled).

* * *

5\. The retailer will look for the vendor in the **Onboarding** page in the portal > click on **View** > **Accept** or **Reject** the **Integration Form**

** **

* * *

6\. If the retailer rejects the form, the vendor will receive an email notification letting them know why it was rejected and will allow them to make adjustments to the form which will be reviewed again the retailer.   
**Status** : Form Open   
If the retailer accepts the form, the vendor account will be deployed to stage and the vendor will receive 2 emails:

  *     * **Welcome to Logicbroker!** \- this email will allow the vendor to create a username and password - it expires in 7 days
    * **Connection with Retailer is ready for testing** \- this email will direct the vendor to the stage portal to begin testing



**Status** : Deployed to Stage

* * *

7\. The vendor will follow along with onboarding documentation provided and complete onboarding tasks. 

* * *

8\. The vendor will reach out to the retailer once they are finished onboarding. The retailer will click on the Go Live button in the portal and the vendor will be deployed to production.
